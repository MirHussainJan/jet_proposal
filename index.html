You already have a live, established website — a valuable starting point, not something to discard. A structured audit decides exactly what stays, what improves, and what gets replaced.

  • Quality images, video & media

06 — Proposed Approach

The Flight Plan

A clear, step-by-step plan — efficient, low-risk, cost-effective. The entire build happens on a separate staging environment, so your live site keeps running until the new one is approved.

Phase 01 · Week 1

Audit, Planning & Architecture

Review the current site, content, media & plugins. Plan pages, menus, URLs and reusable structures. Spin up secure staging.

Phase 02 · Week 2

Theme Setup & Global Styles

Configure the theme/builder, typography, colors and base layout to match the approved design.

Phase 03 · Weeks 2–3

Dynamic Content & Templates

Build editable, structured content areas and modular templates so the team can update without code.

Phase 04 · Weeks 3–5

Page Building & Migration

Build all pages, migrate reusable content, and layer in interactive animations and custom styling.

Phase 05 · Weeks 5–6

Admin Setup, Forms & Responsive

Make every area easy to edit, configure & test forms, streamline plugins, and refine across all devices.

Phase 06 · Weeks 7–8

Performance, SEO & QA

Caching, image optimization & Core Web Vitals. Meta data, sitemaps & 301 redirects. Full cross-browser testing.

Phase 07 · Weeks 8–9

Launch & Handover

Deploy to production with redirects and post-launch checks, then documentation & a live training session for your team.
